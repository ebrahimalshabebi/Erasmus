What is KA171

Erasmus+ beyond Europe's borders.

KA171 is the international dimension of Erasmus+, funding bilateral exchanges between European institutions and partner countries worldwide. Students study or do traineeships abroad; staff teach or train — all with EU financial support.

  • Mobility from 2 to 12 months for students
  • 5 days to 2 months for staff exchanges
  • Monthly grant + distance-based travel support
  • Full ECTS recognition at your home university
Funding Model

Grants designed to make the world reachable.

KA171 funding combines a monthly individual grant with a one-off travel allowance based on the distance to your destination — plus green travel bonuses for low-carbon journeys.

€700 / month grant (student)
€180 / day (staff)
+50€ green travel top-up
