General Information

Not mobility —
cooperation.

Unlike KA1 (mobility), KA2 funds transnational projects. Consortia of at least 3 organisations from 3 different Erasmus+ countries join forces to develop innovative practices, share expertise and deliver tangible outputs with lasting European impact.

Project Journey

From idea to European impact.

Every KA2 project follows a clear lifecycle — from consortium building to dissemination of results.

1

Consortium Building

Identify partners from at least 3 Erasmus+ countries — universities, NGOs, SMEs or research institutions with shared goals.

2

Proposal & Application

Design the project, define objectives and workplan, assemble the budget, and submit the application through the EU Funding & Tenders portal.

3

Project Implementation

Over 12 to 36 months, partners co-develop outputs: curricula, digital tools, studies, training programmes — with regular transnational meetings.

4

Dissemination & Impact

Multiplier events, open access publications and institutional uptake ensure results reach the wider European education community.

Participation

Everyone has a role in KA2.

Students

Contribute to research activities, pilot new tools, join multiplier events and gain international project experience.

Academic Staff

Lead work packages, co-author outputs, manage transnational cooperation and develop new curricula with European peers.

Institutions

Coordinate or partner in consortia, receive EU co-funding, and strengthen institutional capacity through long-term collaboration.
