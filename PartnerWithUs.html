Why Partner With Us

Open doors your students never imagined.

Istanbul Aydın University's Erasmus Office connects institutions worldwide through a proven framework of academic excellence, cultural exchange, and real opportunity.

Pan-European & Global Access

Gain instant connections to 420+ partner institutions across 95+ countries — opening a world of academic opportunities for your students and staff.

Fully Funded Mobility Grants

Travel subsidies, living allowances, and housing support backed by EU and national funding — zero financial burden for participants.

Guaranteed Credit Recognition

ECTS transfer guaranteed across all member institutions. No wasted semesters, fully seamless transcripts and academic continuity.

Research & Staff Collaboration

Co-author papers, share labs, run joint workshops, and send staff on professional development missions across partner universities.

Partnership Process

Becoming an Erasmus+ partner is simple. Follow these four steps to get started.

1

Submit Your Application

Fill out the partnership form with your institution’s details and areas of interest.

2

Evaluation & Review

Our Erasmus Office reviews your application and evaluates compatibility.

3

Agreement Signing

Both institutions sign an inter-institutional agreement to formalize the partnership.

4

Start Mobility

Launch student and staff exchanges, research collaboration, and joint activities.
